# BudgetBuddy Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 28, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how BudgetBuddy handles information when you use the app. 

## Information BudgetBuddy uses

BudgetBuddy lets you enter a profile name, monthly budget, and last 4 digits of the bank account or card details used to identify relevant transaction alerts. If you grant SMS permission, the app reads SMS messages from the date range you select and processes message text, sender, and date on your device to identify transaction amounts and categories. This may include financial information contained in bank or payment messages. SMS access is mandatory; without it, SMS-based transaction features may not work.

The app saves your profile, budget, selected account details, and transaction category data in private app storage on your device. It does not need an online account to provide these features. The app's current implementation does not send this information to a BudgetBuddy server, and the project contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

## How information is used

Information is used to create your budget profile, identify and summarize transactions from selected messages, categorize expenses, display charts, and show your monthly budget in the app and its optional home-screen widget.

## Sharing and service providers

The current app implementation does not share your profile, budget, or SMS-derived transaction information with the app publisher or third parties. This may change if future versions add online services or integrations; the policy should be updated before those features are released.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

App data is stored locally in Android's private app storage. It remains on your device until you remove it in the app (where available), clear the app's storage, or uninstall BudgetBuddy. Uninstalling the app ordinarily removes its private local data. Android device settings and operating-system behavior may also affect local data retention.

## Permissions and your choices

You can decline or revoke SMS permission in Android settings. You can also clear BudgetBuddy's app storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored data. Revoking permission prevents future SMS access but does not itself erase transaction information already saved by the app.

## Children's privacy

BudgetBuddy is a personal finance tool and is not designed for children. The publisher does not knowingly collect children's personal information through an online service.

## Security

BudgetBuddy uses Android's private app storage for its local data. No method of storage is completely secure, and you are responsible for protecting access to your device.

## Changes to this policy

The publisher may update this policy when the app's practices change. The current version and its effective date will be made available with the app or on its distribution page.
