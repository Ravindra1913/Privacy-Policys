This Privacy Policy describes how IRCTC AutoBooker (the “Extension”) handles information when you use it. The Extension helps users complete the ticket booking process on the IRCTC website. The Extension is an independent product of thtek solutions and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC) or Indian Railways.

## 1. How the Extension works

The Extension saves passenger details, booking preferences, and a contact number so you can reuse them for a later booking. When you use it on IRCTC, it uses those saved details to fill booking information and follows the booking options you selected to navigate through the booking flow to the final payment page. You must click the final payment button yourself. The Extension does not make that final payment click for you.

You are responsible for reviewing the passenger and journey details on IRCTC before proceeding. The Extension does not guarantee that a booking will be available, completed, or successful.

## 2. Information handled

The Extension handles the following information that you provide:

- Passenger details entered for booking, such as passenger names and other passenger fields you choose to save.
- Booking preferences used to fill or navigate the booking flow.
- A contact number used as part of the booking details.

The Extension stores these details in the browser for reuse. It uses them only to fill booking details and apply the booking options you selected on IRCTC. It does not use them for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

The Extension does not send the saved passenger details, booking preferences, or contact number to thtek solutions. The Extension does not maintain a server-side account or copy of this saved information.

## 3. Local storage and deletion

Saved information is stored locally by the Extension in your browser and remains available for later use until you remove the Extension. You can delete the saved information by uninstalling IRCTC AutoBooker through your browser’s extension settings. Uninstalling the Extension removes its locally saved data, subject to how your browser handles extension data and backups.

Information you enter or submit on IRCTC is handled by IRCTC under its own policies. Uninstalling the Extension does not delete information already submitted to IRCTC.

## 4. Information sharing and network use

thtek solutions does not receive or sell the locally saved information described in this policy. The Extension does not provide this information to advertising or analytics providers.

When you use the Extension to book a ticket, your browser communicates with IRCTC as part of loading the website and carrying out the booking flow. Information you submit through IRCTC is received and handled by IRCTC under its own privacy policy and terms. Reaching the payment page may involve sending booking information to IRCTC before you decide whether to click the final payment button.

This policy describes the Extension’s handling of saved passenger and booking information. It does not cover information collected directly by your browser, IRCTC, or other services under their own policies.

## 5. Browser permissions

The Extension uses the browser permissions presented during installation and shown in your browser’s extension settings to operate on the IRCTC website and save information for reuse. These permissions support filling booking details and continuing through the booking flow. You can review or revoke permissions in your browser’s extension settings; revoking a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

## 6. Retention

The Extension retains saved passenger details, booking preferences, and contact number locally in your browser until you uninstall the Extension. thtek solutions does not retain a server-side copy. IRCTC determines how long it retains information submitted through its website.

## 7. Security

The saved information is kept in browser storage on your device. Protect your device and browser profile from access by other people, particularly if you use a shared device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure. If you believe information saved by the Extension has been accessed without permission, uninstall the Extension and contact us at the address below.

## 8. Children

IRCTC AutoBooker is intended for people using the IRCTC website to arrange train travel. It is not designed specifically for children. The Extension publisher does not receive the information saved in the Extension. A parent or guardian who has questions about information entered into IRCTC should contact IRCTC directly.

## 9. Your choices and privacy requests

You choose what passenger and booking information to save and use. You can remove the saved information by uninstalling the Extension. You may contact thtek solutions with questions or requests about the Extension’s privacy practices. Since the publisher does not receive or keep a server-side copy of the saved information, requests to access, correct, or delete information submitted to IRCTC should be directed to IRCTC.
